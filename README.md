## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer with a Master's from the University of Florida. I'm passionate about transforming complex datasets into actionable insights and building intelligent solutions that drive impact. With experience at IFAS and Wipro, I've developed expertise in exploratory data analysis, ETL pipeline development, and creating interactive dashboards.

- 🔭 I'm currently working as a **Machine Learning Engineer** at **IFAS**
- 🌱 I'm continuously expanding my knowledge in **Deep Learning** and **Computer Vision**
- 📝 I've published research on **AI for Leaf Wetness Detection** in Sensors 2024
- 👯 I'm looking to collaborate on **Data Science and ML projects**
- 💬 Ask me about **Data Analysis, Machine Learning, and Visualization**
